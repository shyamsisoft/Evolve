## 2. Overview
Organisations don't always need another accountant. Sometimes they need someone who can step back from the numbers, understand the bigger picture and help leadership make better decisions.
We provide CFO and FP&A capability for organisations that need greater financial clarity, stronger planning or additional senior finance expertise.

---

## 3. Beyond reporting the numbers
Effective financial leadership helps answer:
- What is driving our performance?
- Where are we heading?
- Do we have enough cash?
- What risks are emerging?
- Can we afford the next investment?
- Which areas are performing?
- What happens if our assumptions change?
- What should management focus on next?

## 4. CFO Advisory Services
We support leaders across:
- **Financial Strategy**: Translate strategic objectives into practical financial plans.
- **Budgeting & Forecasting**: Build budgets and rolling forecasts that support decision-making.
- **Cash Flow & Working Capital**: Improve visibility and control over liquidity and capital requirements.
- **Board & Stakeholder Reporting**: Provide clear, insightful financial reporting for leadership and boards.
- **Financial Modelling & Decision Support**: Model scenarios, capital investments and commercial opportunities.
- **Governance & Financial Control**: Strengthen oversight, financial discipline and internal processes.
- **Interim CFO & Leadership Cover**: Provide senior capability during growth, transition or recruitment.
- **Business Partnering**: Connect financial outcomes with operational decisions.

## 6. Fractional CFO Support
A fractional CFO provides senior financial leadership without the commitment of a full-time appointment.
This can be valuable when:
- The organisation is growing
- Financial complexity is increasing
- The finance team needs senior support
- Forecasting needs improvement
- Cash flow is becoming difficult to predict
- The organisation is preparing for investment
- The board needs stronger financial reporting
- Leadership needs an independent financial perspective

## 7. When should you consider CFO advisory?
You may benefit when:
- Reports tell you what happened but not why.
- Cash flow is becoming harder to predict.
- Your business is growing faster than your finance function.
- Your board wants better financial insight.
- You are making significant investment decisions.
- Your finance team needs senior-level support.
- You want greater confidence in your forecasts.
