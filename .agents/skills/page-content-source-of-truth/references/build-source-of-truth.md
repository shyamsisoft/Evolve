## 2. Overview
A strong finance function provides more than accurate transactions.
It provides reliable information, efficient processes, effective controls and insight for decision-making.
We help organisations assess, design and improve their finance functions.

---

## 3. Finance Health Check
We assess across five key dimensions:
- **People**: Capability, capacity, roles and responsibilities, leadership, business partnering.
- **Processes**: Month-end, procure-to-pay, order-to-cash, reconciliations, reporting, forecasting.
- **Systems**: ERP utilisation, system configuration, integration, automation, data quality.
- **Reporting**: Management reporting, board reporting, KPIs, budget reporting, forecasting.
- **Controls & Governance**: Financial controls, reconciliations, delegations, compliance, risk.
