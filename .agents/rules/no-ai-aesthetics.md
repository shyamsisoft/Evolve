# Executive Brand Integrity & No AI Aesthetics Rule

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **PERMANENT RULE**: EVOLVE is a practitioner-led executive corporate advisory firm.
> Under **NO circumstances** should any AI agent, assistant, or developer introduce AI-associated icons, animations, or wording into the EVOLVE application.

---

## 1. Zero AI Iconography Standard
- **Never use `<Sparkles />` (✨)** in any component, button, badge, or header.
- **Never use `<Bot />`, `<Wand />`, `<Brain />`**, or any icon suggesting an automated software bot or generative AI.
- **Never use `animate-pulse` on badge icons** (e.g. simulating an AI listening/processing state).

## 2. Permitted Icon Standard
Use authentic brand vector icons (`<PillarIcon type="..." />`) or established corporate advisory Lucide icons:
- **LEAD**: `TrendingUp`, `Compass`, `Target`
- **BUILD**: `Layers`, `Workflow`, `Building2`
- **TRANSFORM**: `Workflow`, `TrendingUp`, `Layers`
- **PROTECT**: `ShieldCheck`, `Shield`, `FileCheck`
- **Advisory / Management**: `Briefcase`, `Users`, `Activity`, `CheckCircle2`
- **CMS / Admin Tools**: `Sliders`, `Settings`

## 3. Zero AI Jargon Standard
- Do not introduce phrases like "AI-powered", "Smart / Intelligent automation", "Copilot", or "Generative".
- Adhere strictly to the human-led practitioner copy outlined in `page-content-source-of-truth`.

Refer to the workspace skill at `.agents/skills/no-ai-aesthetics/SKILL.md` for full implementation guidelines.
